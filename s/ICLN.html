어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund seeks to track the investment results of the index designed to track the performance of approximately 100 clean energy-related companies.
  • The index is a subset of the S&P Global Broad Market Index and includes stocks traded on developed and emerging market exchanges which meet or exceed, at the time of inclusion, $300 million in total market capitalization, $100 million in float-adjusted market capitalization, and $3 million median daily value traded over a six-month period.
  • It is non-diversified.
