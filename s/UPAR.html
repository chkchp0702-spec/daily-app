어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund is an actively-managed ETF that seeks to replicate the returns of the Advanced Research Ultra Risk Parity Index (the “UPAR Index”).
  • The UPAR Index is designed to provide leveraged exposure to the RPAR Index by using an implied financing rate to target 1.4 times the asset class exposures of the RPAR Index at each quarterly rebalance.
  • It is non-diversified.
