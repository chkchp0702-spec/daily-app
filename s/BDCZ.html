  • The ETRACS Wells Fargo® Business Development Company Index ETN Series B due April 26, 2041 are senior unsecured debt securities issued by UBS AG.
  • The index is intended to measure the performance of all business development companies listed on the NYSE or NASDAQ that satisfy specified market capitalization and other eligibility requirements.
  • It refers to the business development companies included in the Wells Fargo® Business Development Company Index as the “index constituents.” The index is a proprietary index of Wells Fargo Securities, LLC.
