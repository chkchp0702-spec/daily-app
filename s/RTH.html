  • The fund normally invests at least 80% of its total assets in securities that comprise the fund's benchmark index.
  • To be initially eligible for the index, companies must generate at least 50% of their revenues from retail.
  • Retail includes companies engaged primarily in retail distribution; wholesalers; online, direct mail and TV retailers; multi-line retailers; specialty retailers; and food and other staples retailers.
  • The fund is non-diversified.
