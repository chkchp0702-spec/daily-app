  • The fund uses a “passive management” approach to track the performance, before fees and expenses, of the index.
  • The index is constructed using a rules-based methodology that identifies companies in the banking industry with publicly traded ordinary shares listed on major stock exchanges in developed European markets, as classified by BITA GmbH.
  • The index’s initial universe consists of all publicly listed equity securities for which sufficient relevant information is available from public sources.
  • The fund is non-diversified.
