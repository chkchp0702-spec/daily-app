어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund is an actively managed exchange-traded fund (“ETF”).
  • Under normal market conditions the fund invests at least 80% of its net assets in debt securities of issuers that are directly involved in digital asset-related activities.
  • The digital asset-related activities that such issuers may be involved in include (i) mining of digital assets; (ii) holding digital assets in their corporate treasury or government treasury; and (iii) working on platforms to facilitate payment with, or the transfer of, digital assets.
