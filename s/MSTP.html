어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund is an actively managed exchange traded fund that attempts to replicate 2 times (200%) daily percentage change of the underlying stock by entering into financial instruments such as swaps and options on the underlying stock.
  • At the end of each trading day, the notional exposure against the underlying stock obtained through the combination of these instruments will be approximately 200% of the fund’s net asset value.
  • The fund is non-diversified.
