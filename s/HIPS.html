  • The fund employs a "passive management"-or indexing-investment approach designed to track the performance of the index.
  • The rules-based index measures the performance of up to 40 high income U.S.-listed securities that typically have "pass-through" structures that require them to distribute substantially all of their earnings to shareholders as cash distributions.
  • This "high income, pass-through" strategy is known as HIPS.
