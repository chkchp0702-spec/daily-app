어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund seeks to track the investment results of the Morningstar® U.S.
  • Dividend and Buyback IndexSM (the “underlying index”).
  • The underlying index is designed to provide exposure to U.S.-based companies that return capital to shareholders through either dividend payments or share buybacks.
  • The underlying index is weighted by total shareholder payout dollars and constrained by capping each company at 4.9%.
  • In addition, the sector weights are constrained within 5% (positive or negative) of the Parent Index.
