  • The fund invests in financial instruments that ProShare Advisors believes, in combination, should track the performance of the index.
  • The index is designed to measure the performance of U.S.-listed companies focused on building the technologies or products that enable the generation of clean energy, such as solar, wind, geothermal, hydrogen, and hydroelectric.
  • The fund will invest in all of the component securities of the index in approximately the same proportion as the index.
  • It is non-diversified.
