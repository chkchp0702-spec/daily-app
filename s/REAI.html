  • The fund is an actively-managed exchange-traded fund (“ETF”) that invest in a diversified portfolio of publicly-traded real estate investment trusts (“REITs”) and mortgage-backed securities (“MBS”) listed primarily on U.S.
  • stock exchanges, and to a lesser extent, stock exchanges in Canada, Europe, and Asia.
  • The fund invests in REITs and MBS that, in the determination of the fund’s sub-adviser, have quantitative and qualitative characteristics that compare favorably to the aggregate real estate portfolio holdings of a select group of public, non-traded REITs.
  • It is non-diversified.
