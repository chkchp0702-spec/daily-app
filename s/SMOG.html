어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund normally invests at least 80% of its total assets in stocks of low carbon energy companies.
  • Such companies may include small- and medium-capitalization companies and foreign issuers.
  • “Low carbon energy companies” refers to companies primarily engaged in renewable energy, including renewable energy production, alternative fuels, electric vehicles, and related technologies and building materials (such as advanced batteries).
  • It is non-diversified.
