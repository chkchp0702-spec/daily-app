어떤 ETF인가

  • The fund seeks to track the investment results of the Cboe FTSE Russell IWM 2% OTM BuyWrite Index (the “underlying index”).
  • The underlying index is designed to measure the performance of holding the iShares Russell 2000 ETF (“IWM” or the “underlying fund”) while writing (selling) call options on the Russell 2000 Index (the “underlying fund’s underlying index” or “RUT”) to generate income.
